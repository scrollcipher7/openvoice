# OpenVoice Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

## Who can use OpenVoice
OpenVoice is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use it.

## What we collect
- Your email address and login details (passwords are handled by our login provider and are not visible to us)
- Profile information you choose to add, such as name, username, bio, hometown, interests, and photos
- What you post and do in the app: posts, photos, links, comments, likes, friend requests, messages, and reports
- Which profiles you view, so people can see who viewed their page. You can set your own viewers list to private in Edit profile
- Basic technical data our hosting providers keep, such as device type and IP address, for security and to keep the service running

## How we use it
We use your information to run OpenVoice, show your content to other members, keep the community safe, review reports, and remove accounts that break the rules. We do not sell your personal information.

## Who can see your content
Posts, comments, and profile information you add can be seen by other OpenVoice members. Messages between friends are private to the people in the conversation, and admins may review content that is reported.

## Services we use
We use Supabase for accounts, the database, and file storage, and Vercel to host the website. They process data on our behalf to run the service.

## Dating and verification
If you use dating features, you may be asked for extra photos or information to confirm you are a real adult. We use it only for that purpose.

## Your choices
You can edit your profile and delete your own posts at any time. To delete your account and the data tied to it, contact us using the details below and we will remove it.

## Rules and reporting
No nudity or sexual content, no threats or illegal content, and no fake profiles. You can report any post, and admins can remove content and ban accounts.

## Contact
Questions or requests, including account deletion: open an issue at https://github.com/scrollcipher7/openvoice/issues. Please do not post private information in a public issue; we will arrange a private way to continue.
